What you’ll learn

01

Where the job is going, and where you stand

The role is shifting from media buyer to Creative Operator. You’ll see what that role actually owns, and which parts of it you are already doing without calling it that.

Live exercisePlace yourself on the map, and name the one gap between you and the role.

02

Why your ads only speak to people who already know you

Most accounts talk to one kind of buyer and never notice. Awareness levels are how you find the ones you have been skipping.

Live exerciseAudit your own ads live, and find the awareness level your account ignores.

03

How a winning ad is actually built

Hook, body, offer: the parts a working ad is made of, and how they fit together, so you can rebuild them on purpose instead of by feel.

Live exerciseBreak down a real ad, so you can model what works instead of copying it.

04

How to turn customer reviews into an insight

“People like the portability” is a finding, and you cannot build an ad on a finding. This difference is what separates an average strategist from a good one.

Live exerciseTake real customer reviews and turn them into an insight you can use.

05

How to get a queue of angles from one insight

One insight should give you more than one ad. This is how you turn it into a list worth testing rather than a single idea you hope lands.

Live exerciseSpin the insight you just built into a set of angles ready to brief.

06

How to tell whether the hook, the body or the offer failed

Read the numbers so your next creative fixes the one thing that broke, instead of changing everything at once and learning nothing.

Live exerciseDiagnose one of your own underperforming ads and decide what to change first.
