FAQ

Is this beginner-friendly?

It's built for people already running or managing Meta ads. You don't need any creative strategy experience. That's what we're building.

Do I need my own ad account?

It helps, since the exercises are designed around your own ads. If you don't have one, you can follow along with the example brand we use throughout the session.

Will there be a replay?

Yes. Attendees get access to the replay for 7 days after the session, so you can go back over the exercises while they're still fresh.

What's your refund policy?

You can request a full refund any time before the workshop starts, no questions asked. Once it has started, all sales are final — seats are limited and the exercise time is reserved for you, so a seat that's used can't be given to someone else.
