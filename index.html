The targeting game is over. Creative is the new targeting.

Meta's algorithm now does most of the work media buyers used to do by hand. Broad audiences, Advantage+, and automated placements mean you can't out-target your competitors anymore.

What's left is the lever that decides whether an account scales or stalls: what you put in front of people.

That's why "Creative Strategist" is one of the most in-demand roles in paid social right now, and why most people trying to fill it are guessing. They copy competitor ads, chase trends, and call it strategy. When an ad wins, they can't explain why. When it dies, they can't fix it.

Creative Strategy Intensive teaches you to stop guessing.

  • ✓You're great in Ads Manager but feel stuck when performance comes down to creative and not settings.

Lifetime. You keep access to the materials, including the recorded versions of Creative Strategy Intensive that come after this batch.

You get 12 months of support, so you can bring real questions from real accounts as you apply the work. It runs in the private community that comes with your seat, so you can post questions there as they come up.

I'm not a media buyer. Can I still join?

Yes. Media buyers will ramp up fastest, but marketers, freelancers, editors, and business owners running their own ads will all get value. Module 1 covers the foundations.

Do I need an active ad account?

No. We use public brands and Meta Ad Library for exercises, so everyone works with the same material. If you do have an account, you can apply everything to it right away.

All sessions are recorded, so you can catch up on anything you miss. You won't get the live critique on the day, so if you know you'll be out, send your questions ahead and they'll be covered in the session.

You can request a full refund any time before the first live session, no questions asked. After the first session starts, all sales are final — seats are limited and live critique time is reserved for you, so a seat that's used can't be given to someone else.
