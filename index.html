The shift

The targeting game is over. Creative is the new targeting.

Meta's algorithm now does most of the work media buyers used to do by hand. Broad audiences, Advantage+, and automated placements mean you can't out-target your competitors anymore.

What's left is the lever that decides whether an account scales or stalls: what you put in front of people.

That's why "Creative Strategist" is one of the most in-demand roles in paid social right now, and why most people trying to fill it are guessing. They copy competitor ads, chase trends, and call it strategy. When an ad wins, they can't explain why. When it dies, they can't fix it.

Creative Strategy Intensive teaches you to stop guessing.

Creative Strategy Intensive is built for you if you are:
