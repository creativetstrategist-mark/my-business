The shift

Creative strategy isn't taste. It's a system.

Good strategists aren't guessing better than you. They're running a loop you can learn: diagnose what the market already believes, name the angle, brief it so it survives production, then design a test that can actually be read.

Diagnose, don't brainstorm

Ideas come from reviews, sales calls and the comment section — not from a whiteboard at 4pm on a Friday.

Separate angle from execution

One angle, five executions. When you keep those apart, a loss tells you which one failed instead of burning both.

Test so it's readable

Structure the test before it launches. Decide what would change your mind, and at what spend, while you can still be honest about it.

Curriculum

Four weeks, in order.

Each week is one live 90-minute session plus a piece of work on your own account. The order matters — every week takes the previous week's output as its input.

Week 01

Diagnose

  • Mine reviews, support tickets and sales calls for the words customers actually use
  • Map what your market already believes, and what it would have to stop believing to buy
  • Audit your last 90 days of creative and find the pattern under the winners
  • You produce: a one-page market diagnosis
Week 02

Angles

  • Turn the diagnosis into named, testable angles — and why naming them is the whole trick
  • Rank angles by market size, proof available and production cost
  • Pressure-test each one against the objection it has to beat
  • You produce: your angle bank, ranked and ready to brief
Week 03

Briefs & production

  • The one-page brief format, and the four fields editors say they actually need
  • Hook libraries: writing ten openings for one angle without repeating yourself
  • Working with UGC creators and editors so the intent survives the edit
  • You produce: three briefs, reviewed live in session
Week 04

Test & read

  • Structuring a test that isolates the angle from the execution
  • Which metric answers which question — hook rate, hold, CTR, CPA, MER
  • Reading noisy results honestly, and setting the stop rule in advance
  • You produce: a 30-day testing plan you run the week after

Questions

Before you enrol

How much time does it take each week?

About four hours: one 90-minute live session, plus roughly two hours on the week's work against your own account. The work is the point — it's the thing you leave with.

I can't make the live sessions in my timezone.

Every session is recorded and posted within a day. You'll miss the live teardown discussion, so send your questions ahead and they'll be covered in the session and the recap.

Is this about media buying or creative?

Creative strategy — what to make and why. We touch the account only where it affects how a test is read. If you want bid strategy and account structure, this isn't it.

Do I need a big budget to get value?

You need enough spend to read a test in a reasonable window — around $20k a month is a sensible floor. Below that the diagnosis and briefing weeks still apply, but results will take longer to confirm.

What if it isn't working for me?

Full refund any time up to the end of week 01, no questions asked. You keep the week 01 materials.

Will you sign an NDA to look at my account?

Yes. Teardowns can also be anonymised for the cohort if you'd rather your brand and numbers stayed out of the session.
