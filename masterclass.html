Why creative is the lever that matters now

Your media buying skills are fine. The problem is that Meta's automation now handles most of the targeting, and the creative itself does the job targeting used to do.

The marketers who'll be most valuable in the next few years aren't the ones with the cleverest campaign structures. They're the ones who know what to make, why it'll work, and how to read the results.

That's creative strategy, and it's a skill you can learn.
